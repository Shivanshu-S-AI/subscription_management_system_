1. SYSTEM ROLE ARCHITECTURE & DATA BOUNDARY MODEL
================================================================================

The Brightloop Software system is structured into three distinct user tiers:

1. ADMIN (Executive & System Management):
   - Unrestricted access across the entire platform.
   - Exclusive control over global configurations: Products, Plans, Tax Rules, 
     Discount Rules, Staff Team Provisioning, and Executive Financial Reports.
   - Master authority over all customer subscriptions, billing cycles, invoices, 
     and payment reconciliation.

2. STAFF / INTERNAL STAFF (Operations & Sales Handling):
   - Designed for daily operational employees (sales, support, billing clerks).
   - Manages customer relationships, quotation generation, quotation conversion, 
     subscription monitoring, manual lifecycle changes, invoice delivery, and 
     payment collection recording.
   - RESTRICTED from modifying global tax rules, global discount rules, recurring 
     plan policies, company catalog pricing, and staff user administration.

3. CUSTOMER (External Subscriber):
   - Self-service access through the Customer Portal.
   - Strict customer-level data isolation: A customer can ONLY view and interact 
     with their own subscriptions, invoices, and profile. Cross-customer access 
     is blocked at both the API layer and the PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) layer.
   - May execute permitted lifecycle actions (pause, resume, renew) only when 
     allowed by the underlying subscription plan policy.

6. END-TO-END CROSS-SYSTEM WORKFLOWS & LIFECYCLE ENGINES
================================================================================

Workflow 1: Sales Deal to Active Subscription
1. Admin configures product catalog, variants, subscription plans, taxes, and discounts.
2. Staff creates a quotation for a customer selecting product packages, quantities, and promo codes.
3. System automatically calculates line subtotals, tax amounts, and applies discounts.
4. Staff confirms quotation and sends it to the customer.
5. Staff converts quotation to subscription:
   - System provisions subscription with unique number (SUB-2026-XXX).
   - Subscription inherits lifecycle policies (auto_close, pause_eligible, early_close_eligible, renewal_eligible).
   - Operation is idempotent: repeated clicks return existing subscription without duplication.
6. Customer immediately sees the active subscription inside their Customer Portal.

Workflow 2: Recurring Billing Cycle & Invoicing
1. Automated Billing Engine evaluates all active subscriptions where next_billing_date <= today.
2. Auto-close check: Subscriptions past expiry_date with auto_close=true are closed immediately.
3. System generates confirmed invoices (INV-2026-XXX) with correct line items, taxes, and due dates.
4. Subscription next_billing_date is advanced to next recurring period.
5. Repeated billing runs on the same day create 0 duplicate invoices (idempotent).
6. Staff and Admin see generated invoices in operational receivables.
7. Customer receives notification and sees invoice in their portal with payment due date.

Workflow 3: Payment Collection & Balance Settlement
1. Customer pays invoice via bank transfer, credit card, or offline payment.
2. Staff or Admin navigates to Payments and records payment against the confirmed invoice.
3. Validation prevents recording amounts greater than the remaining balance.
4. System records payment transaction (PAY-2026-XXX) and deducts amount from invoice balance.
5. When remaining balance reaches $0, invoice status automatically updates to 'paid'.
6. Customer Portal immediately updates invoice status badge to Paid (emerald).

Workflow 4: Customer Self-Service Lifecycle Operations
1. Customer logs into Customer Portal and views their active subscription.
2. Customer clicks "Pause Subscription":
   - System checks subscription plan policy:
     * If pause_eligible = true: Status changes to 'paused', billing pauses, success message shown.
     * If pause_eligible = false: Action rejected with policy error message.
3. Customer clicks "Resume Subscription":
   - Paused subscription transitions back to 'active' status.
4. Customer clicks "Renew Subscription":
   - System verifies renewal_eligible flag and advances next billing period.
